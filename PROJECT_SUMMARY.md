# Project Summary

## Current Goal

Rebuild Aethra Finance as a non-custodial DeFi yield intelligence dashboard.

## Current Stage

MVP repositioning and safety cleanup.

## Confirmed Decisions

- The MVP is an information product, not a fund-custody protocol.
- No token, vault, IEO, revenue share, or automated execution is included.
- Risk scoring must be transparent and explainable.
- Displayed data must include source and update context.

## Archived Material

The earlier protocol, tokenomics, fundraising, grant, and pitch materials are archived because they no longer match the MVP scope and create safety, compliance, or credibility risk.
