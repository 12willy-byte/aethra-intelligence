# Product

## Positioning

Aethra Finance helps DeFi users compare yield opportunities and understand risk before visiting the original protocol.

## Primary User

Experienced DeFi users who already know how to use wallets and protocols, but want a faster way to compare opportunities and risk context.

## Core Workflow

1. Open the dashboard.
2. Filter opportunities by chain, asset, protocol, and risk level.
3. Inspect APY, TVL, update time, source, and risk notes.
4. Save interesting opportunities to a local watchlist.
5. Visit the original protocol if the user wants to continue.

## MVP Pages

- Dashboard
- Opportunity details
- Watchlist
- Data sources
- Risk methodology
- Disclaimer

## Non-Goals

- No deposits
- No portfolio management
- No automatic strategy allocation
- No token incentives
- No investment recommendations
